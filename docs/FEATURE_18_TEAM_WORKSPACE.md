# Feature 18 — Team / Business Workspace

## Scope
- Organizations/workspaces.
- Membership and invitations.
- Roles: owner, admin, member, guest.
- Team projects and shared labels.
- Workspace permissions and audit log.
- Member lifecycle and deactivation.
- Ownership transfer.
- Future-ready entitlement/billing boundary.

## Acceptance criteria
- Workspace data is isolated by workspace ID.
- Owner/admin operations require explicit authorization.
- Deactivated members cannot access workspace resources.
- Ownership transfer is explicit and auditable.
- Guest access is restricted to permitted resources.
